Simple, unbiased access to dense political documents.

Our AI-powered platform allows anyone to engage with important, long and complex political documents which they would otherwise have to rely on others to interpret.

Our Mission

polticialtransparency.ai is a public good. We set up this tool as a not for profit intended to help democratize access and understanding of complex political documents. We hope people use this tool to better their understanding of important documents which define our times, without relying on the potentially bias interpretation of other people, politicians or the media.

How It Works

Find a document

Browse through the documents to find one you want to learn more about.

Have a chat

Use our chat feature to engage with the document, in a format that suits you, without any filter or bias.

Get informed

Receive explanations, insights or references (whatever you want) from the document in whatever format suits you best.

Top tip

The tool is set up to be something between a search engine and a chat bot which is built specifically for each document. You should engage with the tool like it's a chatbot but expect informative and heavily sourced responses, similar to a search engine.
