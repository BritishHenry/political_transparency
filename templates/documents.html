What are the main funding areas in this legislation?

Just now

The Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act allocates funding to several key areas:

  • $110 billion for roads, bridges, and major infrastructure
  • $65 billion for broadband internet access
  • $55 billion for water infrastructure
  • $73 billion for power grid upgrades
  • $39 billion for public transit

These investments aim to modernize America's infrastructure while creating jobs and improving economic competitiveness.

How does this act address climate change?

Just now

The Infrastructure Act includes several climate-focused initiatives:

  • $7.5 billion for a nationwide network of EV charging stations
  • $5 billion for zero-emission buses
  • $47 billion for climate resilience measures to protect against floods, wildfires, and droughts
  • $21 billion for environmental remediation projects
  • Significant funding for clean energy transmission and research

While not primarily a climate bill, these provisions represent the largest federal investment in climate resilience to date.
